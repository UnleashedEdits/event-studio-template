# Owner personalization checklist

Edit `business.js` first; replace every bracketed placeholder before publication.

- [ ] Name, logo, city, confirmed service area, brand colors and final domain.
- [ ] Monitored email, phone, Instagram and WhatsApp number where applicable.
- [ ] One landscape finished-room photo (1800px+), mobile crop (800px), one detail photo (1000px+) and one real team/setup photo with accurate alt text.
- [ ] For each portfolio project: 3–6 approved photos, event category, exact services supplied, description and approved venue/city. Retain licenses, photographer credits and releases. Remove sample projects or replace them, then turn off their inspiration flag.
- [ ] Enable only offered services; confirm deliverables, inventory, equipment limits, operator support and suitable events.
- [ ] Real team names, roles, approach and service-area copy.
- [ ] Client-approved testimonial words, name and context; leave out unverified proof.
- [ ] Availability/confirmation, travel, venue access, setup and collection, equipment handling/damage, deposits/payments, cancellation/rescheduling and custom-quote policies.
- [ ] Approved privacy identity, contact, data use and retention; check email draft delivery to the real recipient.
- [ ] Set final SEO title/description and social image, domain, publication flag, robots rules and sitemap; recheck mobile, keyboard use, both palettes and all contacts on the final host.

The form creates a draft. It does not send a message or confirm a booking. Connect verified delivery before adding a submission-success state.
